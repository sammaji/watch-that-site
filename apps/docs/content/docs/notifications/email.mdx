---
title: Email
description: Configure email notifications to receive email alerts.
---

Email notifications are sent to a list of email addresses when a monitor detects changes. You can provider multiple email addresses (upto 10).

## Setup

1. Go to **Notifications** in your dashboard.
2. In **Email** section, click on "Connect".
3. Add at least one recipient email address. You can add more.
4. Click **Save**.
5. Once saved, enable the email notifications by toggling the switch.

## Adding Multiple Recipients

Click **+ Add more** to add additional email addresses. Each address receives a copy of every notification. You can add up to **10 recipients** per configuration.

When multiple recipients are configured, each email is sent to each recipient. If one recipient fails, the email is still sent to the remaining recipients. Use this to configure backup recipients.

## Troubleshooting

### Not receiving emails

- Check your **spam**folder.
- Verify the email address is correct.
- Make sure email notification is **enabled** (toggle should be on).
