[WatchThatSite](https://wts.sammaji.com) can notify you through multiple channels when a monitor detects a change. Each channel is configured independently and can be enabled or disabled at any time.

## Supported Channels

| Channel                                  | Method                                                                                                                                                                          | What you need                         |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| [Email](/docs/notifications/email)       | SMTP                                                                                                                                                                            | One or more recipient email addresses |
| [Slack](/docs/notifications/slack)       | Incoming Webhooks                                                                                                                                                               | One or more Slack webhook URLs        |
| [Discord](/docs/notifications/discord)   | [Webhooks](/docs/notifications/discord#webhook), [WatchThatSite Bot](/docs/notifications/discord#watchthatsite-bot) or [Custom Bot](/docs/notifications/discord#bot-token-byob) | Varies by method                      |
| [Telegram](/docs/notifications/telegram) | Webhooks                                                                                                                                                                        | One or more Telegram webhook URLs     |

## How It Works

1. **Navigate** to the [Notifications](https://app.wts.sammaji.com/notifications) page in your dashboard.
2. **Click** on Edit under the channel you want to configure.
3. **Fill in** the required credentials (webhook URLs, tokens, email addresses, etc.).
4. **Save** and **Enable** the configuration. Notifications are enabled immediately.

## Managing Configurations

From the [Notifications](https://app.wts.sammaji.com/notifications) page, you can:

- **Enable** or **Disable** a channel without deleting its configuration.
- **Edit** a channel's credentials by clicking the configure button.
- **Disconnect** (delete) a configuration entirely by clicking the delete button.

## Multiple Destinations

Most channels support multiple destinations in a single configuration:

- **Email**: Up to 10 recipient addresses.
- **Slack**: Multiple webhook URLs (each posts to a different Slack channel).
- **Discord Webhooks**: Multiple webhook URLs.
- **Discord Bot**: Multiple channel or thread IDs or user DMs.
