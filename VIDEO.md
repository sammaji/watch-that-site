# Demo video

**Watch it: <https://youtu.be/kK7i3TRI_XM>** (about 3 minutes)

It walks through the full product on a fresh account:

1. **Notifications:** configure where you want to receive notifications (an email address).
2. **Add a Monitor:** add the URL you want to watch and explain, in plain words, what kind of changes to look for. Saving runs the first check, which captures the Baseline and lets the agent decide which parts of the page to watch.
3. **Find a Change:** the price on the page changes, a Run is triggered by hand, and the Change view shows exactly what changed ($99 → $129) with before/after screenshots.
4. **Pre-scraping:** for a page behind a login, describe the steps and add the secrets they need. The agent does them in a real browser, step by step, and records them to replay before every check.
5. **Bring your own keys:** add your own OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Azure or other provider key.
6. **Usage:** see the full breakdown of credit, spend, model calls and tokens.

To try the same journeys yourself, see `AGENTS.md`.
