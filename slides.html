WatchThatSite

Know when the pages you care about change. Not when anything changes.

The problem

Watching a website is either manual, or noisy.

People refresh careers pages, pricing pages and dashboards by hand, because the tools that do it for them can't tell what matters.

Diffs fire on everything

A rotating testimonial, a timestamp, an ad or a reordered list is a "change" to a pixel or text diff. Alerts get ignored.

Rules are brittle

Precise tools ask for CSS selectors and regexes, which break the next time the site ships a redesign.

The pages that matter are behind a sign-in

Billing, account and internal pages need you to sign in, open a tab or pick a filter before there is anything to watch.

Use cases

Job openings

New roles on a careers page, for the team you want.

Prices and plans

A competitor's pricing page, or a plan you pay for.

Back in stock

Availability, sizes or release dates on a product page.

Policies and terms

Changes to terms, privacy policies or documentation.

Official notices

Results, tenders, appointment slots or announcements.

Behind a login

Billing, account or internal dashboards that need a sign-in.

How it works

An agent that reads the page like you would.

It only spends a model call when something actually moved.

1

Finds what matters

The first check splits the page into parts and decides, from your instructions, which to watch and which are Noise.

2

Compares the text

Each watched part's words are diffed against the Baseline, so a changed price or a new role shows up exactly.

3

Compares the looks

Screenshots are compared pixel by pixel for parts watched for how they look: images, charts, layout.

4

Judges it

When something differs, the agent weighs it against your instructions and writes a summary with the evidence.

5

Records the way in

Behind a login, it does your steps once in a real browser, records them, and repairs a step when the site changes.

Nothing moved

The check ends quietly. No model call, no alert, nothing to read.

It matters to you

You get the summary, the changed words and before/after screenshots, on your channels.

The details

Built to keep working when the page changes.

Self-repairing steps

Pre-scraping replays as plain Playwright with no model. If the site renames a tab, the agent repairs the step.

Secrets stay secret

Passwords are encrypted and typed by the browser. The model only sees {{secret:password}}.

Noise filtered early

Volatile parts are marked once, then cut from later diffs before they reach the model.

One key per account

A virtual key carries the credit as a budget and holds the account's own provider keys.

Private by default

Row-level security for data, short-lived signed URLs for screenshots.

A clean browser

Each check runs in a fresh browser, so nothing leaks between Runs or accounts.
